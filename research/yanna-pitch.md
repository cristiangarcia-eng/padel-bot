# YANNA

### Every birthday is a building block.

---

## THE PROBLEM

Every year, **$25 billion** is spent on children's birthday gifts in the US. Most of it ends up forgotten, broken, or outgrown within months. Meanwhile, the average American family has less than $1,000 saved for their child's future.

Parents know they should invest for their kids. But the 73 million parents in the US face the same friction: opening a custodial account feels intimidating, and asking grandma to "invest instead of gifting" feels awkward.

**There's no culturally natural moment to start investing for a child.** Until now.

---

## THE SOLUTION

**Yanna** turns every birthday into a wealth-building moment — through viral, shareable Birthday Pages that make financial gifting as easy and emotional as sending a card.

**One link. Shared once. Seen by dozens.**

A parent creates a Birthday Page in 60 seconds. Guests land on a beautiful, personalized page where they can:

1. **Leave a video message** (or photo + text)
2. **Gift any amount** ($5 minimum) — invested automatically
3. **See the projected value** at age 18 ("Your $50 could become $195")

The child gets a time capsule of love AND a growing portfolio. No wrapping paper. No waste. No awkwardness.

---

## WHY BIRTHDAY PAGES GO VIRAL

| Mechanic | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Shared via group chat, social, email** | Each page reaches 15-40 guests organically |
| **Social proof** | "12 people have already gifted" triggers FOMO |
| **Projected growth** | "$50 → $195 by age 18" makes gifting feel powerful |
| **Video messages** | Emotional content people actually want to watch and share |
| **Annual recurrence** | Birthdays happen every year — built-in retention loop |
| **Zero friction for guests** | No app download, no account needed to gift |

**Every birthday party becomes a distribution event.**

A single child's page reaches ~25 adults. If 5 of them have kids, they create their own pages. That's **5x organic growth per cycle** with zero ad spend.

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW

### For Parents (Account Holders)
- Create UGMA custodial account in < 3 minutes
- Choose from 5 managed portfolios (ETF-based, partnered with BlackRock/Vanguard)
- Recurring investments from $5/month
- Digital time capsule with all birthday messages, photos, videos
- Multi-child support under one subscription

### Birthday Pages (The Growth Engine)
- Custom URL: `yanna.co/emma-turns-5`
- Branded, beautiful, mobile-first landing page
- Real-time guest contributions with social proof counter
- Compound interest projector for each gift
- Video/photo messages from every guest
- Shareable via link, QR code, or embed
- Printable QR cards for physical party invites
- **Annual auto-renewal**: page refreshes each year with updated age and new theme

### For Guests (No Account Needed)
- Land on page, watch intro video from parents
- Choose amount, leave message, pay via Apple Pay / Google Pay / card
- Done in under 90 seconds
- Receive thank-you video from the child/parents after the party

### Beyond Birthdays (Expansion)
- Baby showers, graduations, holidays, first day of school
- Registry integrations (Amazon, Babylist)
- Milestone pages (first steps, lost tooth, recital)

## THE VISION

EarlyBird proved that families want to invest together. But they built a product that required convincing people to download an app.

**Yanna flips the model.** We don't ask anyone to download anything. We meet families where they already are — at the birthday party. Every candle blown out is a portfolio growing. Every guest who gifts $25 becomes a potential parent who creates their own page next month.

**We're not building an investing app. We're building the new birthday card.**

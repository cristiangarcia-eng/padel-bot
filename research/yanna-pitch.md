# YANNA

### 18 years of compound interest. One account. Total freedom.

---

## THE PROBLEM

An 18-year-old in America starts adult life with an average of **$0 in investments and $1,600 in savings.** Meanwhile, they face decisions that will define the next 50 years: college, a trade, a business, travel, a gap year. Most can't choose freely — they choose what they can afford.

Here's the math no one is doing for them:

> **$100/month invested from birth at 8% annual return = $48,000 at age 18.**
> **$200/month = $96,000.** Enough to choose their life instead of settling for one.

The money exists. American families spend **$25 billion/year on children's birthday gifts** — toys that break, clothes that shrink, gift cards that expire. What if even a fraction of that went into an account that compounds for 18 years?

The problem isn't that parents don't care. **The problem is that no product makes it effortless to build wealth for a child from day one.**

---

## THE VISION

**Yanna exists so that every child turns 18 with real financial freedom.**

Not a trust fund. Not a college-only 529. A **flexible investment account** that grows silently for 18 years through the power of compound interest — funded by the people who love the child most.

At 18, the child inherits the account and decides: college, start a business, travel the world, invest in real estate, or keep growing it. **No restrictions. Their money. Their choice. Their life.**

## HOW IT WORKS

### The Account: A UGMA That Grows For 18 Years

A parent opens a custodial investment account (UGMA) in under 3 minutes. Funds are invested in diversified ETF portfolios and **compound automatically for the child's entire childhood.**

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Account type | UGMA — child legally owns the assets |
| Investment options | 5 managed portfolios (Conservative → High Growth) via BlackRock iShares ETFs |
| Expected return | 6-10% annually (S&P 500 historical avg: 10.67%) |
| Minimum to start | $5 |
| Recurring investments | From $5/month (weekly or monthly) |
| Rebalancing | Automatic quarterly rebalancing |
| Tax advantage | First $1,250 of child's unearned income = tax-free |
| SIPC protection | Up to $500,000 per account |
| No restrictions on use | Unlike 529 plans — funds can be used for anything at 18 |
| Transfer | Automatic at age 18/21 (state dependent) |

### The Math That Changes Everything

**The key insight:** A family doesn't need to be wealthy. They need to **start early and be consistent.** $100/month is $3.33/day. Yanna makes it easy for not just parents, but grandparents, uncles, godparents, and friends to contribute together.

## THE GROWTH ENGINE: BIRTHDAY PAGES

The hardest part of building wealth for a child isn't the investing — it's getting the whole family involved. Yanna solves this with **Birthday Pages**: viral, shareable gift pages that turn every birthday into a wealth-building event.

**One link. Shared once. Seen by dozens.**

A parent creates a Birthday Page in 60 seconds → shares it in the group chat → guests land on a beautiful page where they:

1. **See the child's account growing** in real time (social proof)
2. **Gift any amount** ($5+) — invested automatically into the child's portfolio
3. **Watch what their $50 becomes** — "$50 today → $195 at age 18" (compound interest projector)
4. **Leave a video message** — stored forever in the child's digital time capsule

### Why This Goes Viral

| Mechanic | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **No app download for guests** | Gift via web in 90 seconds (Apple Pay / Google Pay) |
| **Social proof counter** | "14 people have already gifted $820" — triggers action |
| **Compound interest projector** | Every gift feels 3-4x more impactful when you see the future value |
| **Annual recurrence** | Birthdays happen every year — the page auto-renews |
| **Emotional video messages** | Content people want to watch, save, and share |
| **Network effect** | Each page reaches ~25 adults → ~5 have kids → they create their own pages |

**Every birthday party becomes a distribution event.** CAC drops to ~$8 (organic) vs. $35-50 industry average.

| Age | What happens |
